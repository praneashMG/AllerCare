Core Immunotherapy Services

Two Proven Paths to Long-Term Tolerance

Our clinic specializes in the two most clinically validated forms of allergen immunotherapy. Your allergist will recommend the right path based on your age, lifestyle, and allergy profile.

SCIT
Subcutaneous Immunotherapy

Allergy Shots (SCIT)

Regular injections of gradually increasing allergen doses, administered in our clinic under physician supervision. Typically involves weekly build-up visits followed by monthly maintenance.

  • Clinically proven for pollen, dust mite, pet dander, and venom allergies
  • In-clinic administration with 30-minute observation protocol
  • Long-term remission possible after 3–5 years of therapy
Learn About SCIT
Sublingual Immunotherapy

Under-the-Tongue Drops (SLIT)

A needle-free alternative where allergen extract is placed under the tongue daily at home. Ideal for patients who prefer to avoid injections or have busy schedules.

  • No injections—self-administered daily at home
  • Excellent safety profile with reduced risk of severe reactions
  • Particularly effective for pollen and dust mite allergies

Your Treatment Timeline

From First Visit to Lasting Freedom

Immunotherapy is a commitment—but it's one with a clear, predictable roadmap. Here's exactly what your journey looks like at our clinic.

Week 1

Comprehensive Evaluation

Detailed history, skin prick testing, and IgE blood work to identify your exact allergen profile.

Weeks 2–20

Build-Up Phase

Gradual dose escalation to reach your therapeutic target—with in-clinic monitoring for SCIT or home dosing for SLIT.

Months 6–36

Maintenance Phase

Consistent dosing at your target level. Most patients report significant symptom reduction by month 6.

Year 3–5

Graduation & Lasting Freedom

Many patients achieve sustained tolerance and can discontinue therapy—with long-term symptom relief.
