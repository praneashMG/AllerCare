Diagnostic Testing

Precision Allergy Testing Services

Accurate diagnosis is the foundation of effective treatment. We use the latest in vivo and in vitro testing methods to pinpoint your exact triggers—quickly and comfortably.

Skin Prick Testing

Rapid testing for up to 40 inhalant and food allergens with immediate wheal-and-flare results in 15 minutes.

Specific IgE Blood Testing

Quantitative blood analysis for precise allergen-specific IgE levels—ideal for patients who cannot undergo skin testing.

Patch Testing

T.R.U.E. test and extended patch panels for contact dermatitis, cosmetics, metals, and chemical sensitivities.

Oral Food Challenge

Physician-supervised graded food challenges to confirm or rule out food allergies in a controlled clinical setting.

Spirometry & Lung Function

Objective airway assessment for allergic asthma diagnosis and monitoring, integrated with your allergy profile.

Venom & Drug Allergy Testing

Specialized protocols for stinging insect venom and antibiotic/medication allergy evaluation and management.

Immunotherapy Treatments

Long-Term Desensitization Therapy

Unlike medications that temporarily suppress symptoms, immunotherapy retrains your immune system to tolerate allergens over time—offering lasting relief and potentially preventing new allergies from developing.

SCIT
Subcutaneous Immunotherapy

Allergy Shots (SCIT)

Regular injections of gradually increasing allergen doses, administered in our clinic under physician supervision. Typically involves weekly build-up visits followed by monthly maintenance.

  • Clinically proven for pollen, dust mite, pet dander, and venom allergies
  • In-clinic administration with 30-minute observation protocol
  • Long-term remission possible after 3–5 years of therapy
Book SCIT Consultation
Sublingual Immunotherapy

Under-the-Tongue Drops (SLIT)

A needle-free alternative where allergen extract is placed under the tongue daily at home. Ideal for patients who prefer to avoid injections or have busy schedules.

  • No injections—self-administered daily at home
  • Excellent safety profile with reduced risk of severe reactions
  • Particularly effective for pollen and dust mite allergies

Compare Treatment Options

SCIT vs. SLIT: Which Is Right for You?

Both forms of immunotherapy are effective, but they differ in administration, convenience, and candidacy. Your allergist will help you choose based on your lifestyle, age, and allergy profile.

Feature SCIT (Shots) SLIT (Drops)
Administration In-clinic injection At-home under-tongue drops
Frequency Weekly build-up, then monthly Daily at home
Clinic Visits Frequent during build-up Minimal—periodic check-ins
Best For Pollen, dust mite, pet dander, venom Pollen, dust mite; needle-averse patients
Age Range Adults & children 5+ Adults & children 5+
Insurance Coverage Widely covered Coverage varies by plan
Duration 3–5 years typical 3–5 years typical

Not sure which option fits your needs? Our allergists can help you decide during a consultation.
